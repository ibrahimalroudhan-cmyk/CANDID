# Code of Conduct

Be courteous and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks
and discrimination are not tolerated in issues, discussions or any other space
connected to this project.

Never post patient data, scans or anything that could identify a patient.
Such posts will be deleted.

Concerns can be reported privately to
[Ibrahim@candidlabs.org](mailto:Ibrahim@candidlabs.org). Maintainers may remove
comments or block users who breach these expectations.
